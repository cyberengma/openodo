# OpenOdo Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 2026

OpenOdo is a private, offline-first vehicle log. This policy explains what
data the app handles and how it is stored.

## Summary

OpenOdo **does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data**. The app
has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, and
no network access (`INTERNET` permission is not declared). Everything you
enter stays on your device.

## What the app stores

OpenOdo stores the information you enter, locally on your device:

- Vehicle details (name, make, model, year, VIN, units, currency, notes).
- Fuel and charging entries (dates, odometer, volume/energy, cost, station).
- Maintenance and expense records (service types, costs, shops, notes).
- Service reminders.
- Receipt references: if you attach a receipt, the app keeps a link to the
  image/PDF file you selected on your device. The file itself is not copied,
  uploaded, or shared.

## How data is used

Your data is used only to display your own log and statistics, and to schedule
local maintenance reminders. All calculations happen on the device.

## Data sharing

None. OpenOdo never sends your data anywhere, and there is no third-party code
that could. There are no servers operated by OpenOdo.

## Backups and exports

You may export your data as a JSON backup or CSV file, which you choose where
to save. Exports are initiated by you and remain under your control.

## Deleting your data

All data is stored locally. Clear the app's storage (or uninstall the app) to
permanently remove it. You can also delete individual vehicles, records, and
reminders from within the app.

## Permissions

OpenOdo requests no sensitive permissions. It does not request internet,
location, contacts, or microphone access.

If this policy changes, the update will be reflected here.
