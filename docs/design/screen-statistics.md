# Statistics

Provide date range and liquid/electric filters. Show cards for L/100 km,
km/L, US MPG, UK MPG, kWh/100 km, mi/kWh, cost per distance, best/worst
valid spans, monthly cost/volume/energy, and unit price by fuel label.

Charts must have a textual summary and explicit units. Missed-fill and
invalid spans are excluded with an explanation. Empty and insufficient-data
states distinguish “no entries” from “not enough valid spans.”
