OpenOdo 0.1.0 — a private, offline vehicle log.

• Log fuel and electric charging; enter quantity plus price or total and the
other fills in automatically.
• Unified fuel, service, repair, and upgrade history with multi-service expenses.
• Maintenance reminders that reset automatically when work is logged.
• Statistics: total cost, fuel economy, and per-category expense breakdowns.
• Import Drivvo / Fuelio CSV, JSON backup & restore, CSV export.
• Light and dark themes.
